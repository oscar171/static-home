How SynapticSpark works

Connect the social channels you already manage and let AI help you scale community management, content, and reporting — without changing platforms.

1

Connect your accounts

Securely link the Facebook Pages and Instagram Business accounts you (or your clients) already manage. We use the official Meta APIs and OAuth — you stay in control of every permission.

  • Facebook Pages
  • Instagram Business accounts
  • WhatsApp Business
2

AI handles the heavy lifting

Our AI agents read incoming messages and comments, classify sentiment, suggest replies, and help you publish optimized content across every connected channel.

  • Unified inbox for messages & comments
  • AI sentiment analysis & smart routing
  • Content scheduling & publishing
3

Measure & report from one dashboard

Track engagement, reach and response times across all your accounts in real time, and export branded reports for your stakeholders or clients.

  • Cross-channel performance metrics
  • Branded client reports
  • Role-based access for teams

Built on the official Meta Platforms APIs

SynapticSpark uses Meta's Graph API and Instagram Graph API in compliance with the Meta Platform Terms. Your customers' tokens are encrypted at rest, never sold, and never used to train AI models.
